# Reach — Google Play store listing copy

## App name (30 char max)
Reach

## Short description (80 char max)
Private messenger that finds the people right around you. No number needed.

## Full description (4000 char max)
Reach is a private messenger built around one idea: the people near you.

No phone number. No email. No name required. Your identity is a key created on
your own device — you exist the moment you install, and you're findable by an
8-digit ID or by simply being nearby.

RADAR — SEE WHO'S AROUND YOU
Open the Radar and the people running Reach in Bluetooth range appear as live
dots. Same room, same street, same festival. It works with zero internet —
pure Bluetooth — so you can find and message people even with no signal at all.

CLINK — TAP PHONES TO SWAP CONTACTS
Meet someone in person? Hold your phones together and tap. Both of you are
saved, instantly, mutually. No QR codes, no typing IDs, no internet.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Every message is end-to-end encrypted. Our server only ever sees ciphertext it
cannot read — no message content, no tracking, no ads, no analytics, nothing
sold. Turn off "Visible to others" and only your contacts can reach you.

EVERYTHING A MESSENGER SHOULD HAVE
• Text, photos, and voice notes
• Encrypted voice and video calls
• Reactions, replies, and delete-for-everyone
• Groups — including blind groups where members never see each other's
  identities, and announcement groups where only the admin posts
• 24-hour status
• Encrypted message backup, so a new phone restores your history

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Bluetooth mesh means messages find a path even when the network doesn't.
On a mountain, on a plane, in a blackout — Reach still reaches.

Reach is for the people around you. Download it, and see who's nearby.

## Release notes — first release (500 char max)
The first release of Reach. Find people around you on the Radar, tap phones to
swap contacts with Clink, and message privately with full end-to-end
encryption — text, photos, voice notes, and calls. Works over Bluetooth with
no internet. We'd love your feedback.
